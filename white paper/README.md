## 1. Abstract

Shadow Diamondz operates a multi-chain DeFi and entertainment ecosystem spanning Arbitrum One, DiamondzChain (an Arbitrum Orbit AnyTrust L3, Chain ID 7791), with planned expansion to Solana and Hyperliquid. The ecosystem centers on three pillars:

1. **Shadow Peoples Vault** — A multi-strategy basket vault where users deposit USDC and receive diversified crypto exposure plus Aave V3 yield, governed by an on-chain DAO.
2. **DiamondzChain Bridge** — A cross-chain lock-mint-burn-unlock bridge with 6 wrapped zwTokens, secured by 2-of-3 validator consensus.
3. **SDM Token Economy** — A multi-token system (SDM, vSDM, wSDM, gSDM, sSDM, DBV, CRABBY) with revenue flywheel that deepens ecosystem liquidity through automated SDM buyback and DODO LP seeding.

All protocol revenue flows through a self-reinforcing flywheel: deposits → fees → SDM buyback → DODO liquidity → stronger ecosystem → more deposits.
